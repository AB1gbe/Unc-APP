# ADR-050 Terms of use

See [[Deals, Terms, Cards and Installments#3. Terms of use]].

| Question | Options | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The key rule | Nothing; **"deals agreed on Unc are finished on Unc"** | The rule | What the owner asked for; Upwork and Airbnb have similar rules. |
| What "finished on Unc" means today | Pay through Unc (not possible yet); **keep arrangements in the chat, mark it found, use Unc payments when offered** | Today's version | Fair now; ready for payments later. |
| Consequences | Fines now; **warn, remove badges, limit or close, and charge the fee once Unc charges fees** | Steps | Proportionate; no fee exists yet. |
| Agreeing | A link in the footer; **a tick box at sign-up + one-time banner for existing members + a reminder at Accept** | Clear agreement | Terms only count if people clearly agreed, and the reminder comes at the moment that matters. |
| Versions | One-off; **`TERMS_VERSION`** | Versioned | Changing the Terms asks everyone again. |
| Legal status | Final; **draft, marked for a Bahrain lawyer** | Draft | Claude is not a lawyer; PDPL and consumer law need checking. |

**Revisit when:** a lawyer reviews it (then remove the draft note and raise `TERMS_VERSION` if the wording changes), or Unc starts charging fees.
