# ADR-047 Report follow-up and raise my budget

See [[Your Data, Dashboard and Care#5. Report follow-up and raise my budget]].

| Question | Options | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telling the reporter | Nothing; full details; **"took action" or "found nothing"** | Outcome only | Reports feel worth it; the other person's privacy is kept. |
| If the admin gives no outcome | Assume one; **tell nobody** | Tell nobody | Never send a wrong message. The admin screen always sends one. |
| Raising the budget | Edit the request; **a one-tap "Raise my budget"** | One tap | Quick, and it can tell the right sellers. |
| Who is told | Everyone; alerts; **sellers who looked at or saved it, haven't offered, and may answer** | Interested sellers | They already showed interest; no spam. |
| How often | Any time; **once a day** | Once a day | Stops buyers from nudging sellers again and again. |
| How loud | Push; **🔔 only** | 🔔 | Not urgent. |

**Revisit when:** reporters ask for more detail, or sellers say budget nudges are too many.
