# ADR-049 Did you get it

See [[Deals, Terms, Cards and Installments#2. Did you get it]].

| Question | Options | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| When | 1 day; **3 days after accepting**; a week | 3 days | Enough time to meet; still fresh. |
| Who | Both; **the buyer** | Buyer | Only the buyer marks "found it". |
| How often | Repeat; **once** | Once | A reminder, not nagging. |
| How loud | Push; **🔔 only** | 🔔 | Not urgent. |

**Revisit when:** many accepted prices still end without "found it" (then also ask the seller).
