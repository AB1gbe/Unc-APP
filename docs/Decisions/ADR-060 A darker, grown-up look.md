# ADR-060 A darker, grown-up look

See [[Design System#The new look (session 62)]].

| Question | Options | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theme | Light (old, with dark following the phone); **dark for everyone**; both with a switch | Dark | The owner wants a look like Siin and their example: dark, calm, premium. One look is simpler to keep right. |
| Accent | Blue (old); purple (Siin); **warm coral** | Coral | Matches the owner's example ("See all" in orange), stands out on dark, and isn't a copy of Siin. Dark text on coral keeps contrast. |
| Fonts | System fonts (old); Google Fonts links; **bundled Fraunces + Inter + Noto Kufi Arabic** | Bundled | Elegant serif headings; no requests to outside sites (privacy, works offline). |
| Pictures | Text only (old); bright cartoon scenes (first try, "too childish"); **one object on a deep muted colour**; real stock photos | One object | Calm and consistent, no rights issues. Stock photos couldn't be downloaded here; real member photos take over in use. |
| Cards with no photo | Text only; **category picture** | Category | Every card gets an image, so lists don't read as walls of text. |

**Revisit when:** Unc has real photos to show on the front page (shop photos, finished deals), or members ask for a light mode.
