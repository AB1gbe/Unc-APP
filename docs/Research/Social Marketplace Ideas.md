# Ideas: make Unc feel like a social marketplace, not a robotic app

Asked in [[2026-10-21 Session 19]]. These are ideas only: nothing is built yet. Each one keeps the MUST: **people contact each other only about a specific request** ([[01 Project Brief]]). Ideas that come close to that line are marked ⚠️ and need the owner's decision.

**Effort:** S = a day or less · M = 2–4 days · L = a week or more.

## 1. Sound like people, not a system (quick wins)
| Idea | What it looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Human wording everywhere** | "Request fulfilled" → "Found it! 🎉"; "No results" → "Nobody's asked for that yet. Be the first?"; "Offer sent" → "Sent! Riverside usually replies within an hour." | S |
| **Greeting by time of day and season** | "Good evening, Sam" · "Ramadan Kareem 🌙" · "Eid Mubarak" banners, local holidays | S |
| **Celebrate moments** | Small confetti when a deal is done, after the first request, and after the first 5★ rating | S |
| **Loading placeholders instead of "Loading…"** | Grey card shapes that fill in, so the app feels fast and alive | S |
| **Friendly empty states with a next step** | Every empty page suggests one action, with an illustration | S |

## 2. Show that real people are here (social proof)
| Idea | What it looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **"Happening near you" feed** on Home | "Someone in Riffa found a PS5 · 2h ago", "3 shops answered requests in Manama today". No names unless the person agreed. | M |
| **Seen by sellers** on a request | "👀 7 sellers looked at this · 2 made offers": the buyer sees interest growing instead of silence | S |
| **"Active now" / "Replies within ~1 hour"** on profiles and in chats | Computed from message times; turns a name into a person you can expect an answer from | S–M |
| **"Found it" success stories** | After a deal, both sides can share a photo and a sentence ("Got my bike, thanks Ali! 🚲"). Shown on Home and both profiles. Opt-in. | M |
| **Live counters on the landing page** | "1,240 requests found what they were looking for" | S |

## 3. Profiles that feel like people and shops
| Idea | What it looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Shop pages** for business accounts | Cover photo, specialities (chips), area on a map, opening hours, "Deals done", gallery from success stories | M |
| **Badges** | ✓ Verified (exists) · ⚡ Fast responder · 🏆 Top finder (10+ deals, 4.5★+) · 🤝 Trusted buyer · 🌱 New member. Earned automatically, never bought. | M |
| **Thank-you notes on ratings**, with an optional photo of what was received | Reviews become stories | S–M |

## 4. Make the conversation feel like talking, not filling in forms
| Idea | What it looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer cards with buttons** | An offer shows as a card with **Accept**, **Counter** and **Not for me**. "Counter" sends a new price in one tap. | M |
| **Quick replies** | Tap-to-send chips: "Is it still available?", "Can you send more photos?", "Where can we meet?" | S |
| **Reactions on messages** | 👍 ❤️ 😂 🙏 long-press on phones | S–M |
| **Typing indicator** ("Riverside is typing…") | Uses the existing 4-second polling, or real-time later | S–M |
| **Voice notes** | Very common in Bahrain (WhatsApp habit). Private like chat photos. | L |

## 5. Grow through people's own networks
| Idea | What it looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Share a request to WhatsApp / Instagram / X** | A button, plus a nice preview card (the request's photo, title and budget) when the link is pasted | S–M |
| **Invite a shop to your request** | "Know a shop that might have it? Send them this request" → a WhatsApp link that opens the request. The contact still happens through the request. | S |
| **Favourite sellers** | ♥ a seller you liked. When you post a new request in their category, they get notified first ("A past customer is looking for…"). They still answer through that request. | M |

## 6. Community around places and interests
| Idea | What it looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **City / area pages** | "Manama: trending requests, top finders this month" | M |
| **"Me too" on a request** | Other buyers tap "I want this too": sellers see demand is bigger, and each "me too" buyer can turn it into their own request in one tap (copy). | M |
| ⚠️ **Public questions on a request** | Sellers ask "Which colour?" publicly; the buyer answers once for everyone. It is about the request, but it is public, not private: **needs the owner's decision.** | M |
| ⚠️ **Following members** | Following a member to see their new requests. It doesn't allow messaging, but it moves toward a social network: **needs the owner's decision.** | M |

## Recommended first five (most "human" for the least work)
1. **Human wording + celebrations + loading placeholders** (all of section 1): one pass over the app, about 1–2 days.
2. **Offer cards with Accept / Counter + quick replies**: makes the main moment of the app feel like a conversation.
3. **"Active now", reply time and "seen by sellers"**: shows there are people on the other side.
4. **Share to WhatsApp, with preview cards**: how things spread in Bahrain ([[Bahrain Market Research]]).
5. **"Found it" success stories + badges**: proof that it works, and a reason to come back.

## Never (breaks the MUST or trust)
- Direct messages between profiles, or a message button on profiles.
- Showing who viewed your profile by name.
- Paid badges, or "verified" that can be bought.
- Fake activity ("5 people are looking at this") that isn't real.
