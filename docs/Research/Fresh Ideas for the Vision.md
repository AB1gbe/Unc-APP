# Fresh ideas that fit the vision (session 33)

The vision: **buyers post what they want; verified shops and finders contact them privately, only about that request.**
This list avoids repeating [[Social Marketplace Ideas]] (session 19) and [[More Ideas for the Vision]] (session 30). Every idea keeps the three MUSTs: proper KYC, simple design, and contact only inside a request.

Effort: **S** = a few hours · **M** = a day or two · **L** = several days. ⚖️ = the owner must decide something first (money, law, an outside service).

## 1. New kinds of requests
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shopping-list request** | One request with several items ("moving in: fridge, sofa, 2 beds"). Sellers can offer on **some** of the items; the buyer sees which items are covered | Newcomers and people moving house need many things at once; one post instead of five | M–L |
| **Borrow or rent** | A request type "Rent / borrow" with dates ("projector for Friday night"). Offers show a price per day | Many needs are short-term; nobody else in Bahrain does "wanted to rent" | M |
| **Swap** | "I'll trade my PS4 + BD 50 for a PS5". The request shows what the buyer gives in return | Second-hand culture; still one request, one private chat | S–M |
| **Group request ("we need 20")** | A school class, office or majlis posts one request for many of the same thing; shops offer a bulk price | Shops love bulk orders; buyers get better prices | M |
| **For someone else** | "Posting for my mother in Riffa": the request shows the area where the item is needed, not the poster's | Families in Bahrain often shop for parents; simple field | S |
| **Giving requests (Ramadan / charity)** | "Need a wheelchair, can't pay much". Sellers can offer free or at cost; a 🤲 tag. Only verified members, as now | Strong local giving culture, especially in Ramadan; makes Unc feel human | M |

## 2. Privacy that makes people comfortable posting
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First-name-only until you reply** | Sellers see "Sam B. in Juffair"; the full name shows only after the buyer answers an offer | Many people (especially women) hesitate to post with their full name | S |
| **Who may contact me** | Per request: "anyone verified" (default), "only shops", "only sellers with 4★+", or "only my favourite sellers" | The buyer controls who knocks; fits "only about this request" perfectly | M |
| **Hidden budget** | Tick "Don't show my budget": sellers still see the category's usual range, and offers above it are flagged to the buyer only | Stops everyone offering exactly the maximum | S |
| **Quiet hours** | "No alerts 11 pm–7 am" and an optional pause around Friday prayer. Notifications wait and arrive together | Respectful; fewer people turn notifications off completely | S–M |

## 3. Trust you can see
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Proof photo with a code** | When a seller sends a photo of the item, Unc can ask for a "proof photo" showing a short code written on paper (e.g. **UNC-4821**). A ✓ Proof badge shows on the photo | The classic way to stop stolen-photo scams, with no AI needed | S–M |
| **Condition words everyone shares** | New · Like new · Good · Fair, each with a one-line meaning, picked on the offer | Fewer arguments at pickup ("you said good!") | S |
| **Warranty and returns on the offer** | Tick boxes: "3-month warranty", "Return within 3 days", "No returns" — shown on Compare offers | Shops can stand out by promise, not only price | S |
| **Offer valid until** | "This price is valid until Saturday"; after that the offer card greys out | Honest for shops whose prices change; adds gentle urgency | S |
| **"Something went wrong" after a deal** | A help button on the finished deal: item not as described / no-show / other. Goes to the admin with the chat attached (admins already see reports) | A clear place to complain builds trust in the whole site | M |
| **Polite closing note to the other sellers** | When the buyer marks the request found, sellers who made offers get: "Sam found it elsewhere — thanks for your offer 🙏" | Sellers today just see a closed chat; this feels human and fair | S |

## 4. Help shops grow (without ads)
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unmet demand board** | For verified shops: "Asked for this month, few offers: PS5 discs (14 requests), car seat covers (9)". Counts only, no names | The real power of a reverse marketplace: shops learn what to stock | M |
| **Shop QR sticker** | Each shop page gets a printable QR poster: "Can't find it on our shelf? Ask on Unc". Scans open the shop page | Brings shops' own walk-in customers onto Unc | S |
| **Team accounts for shops** | A shop owner invites 1–3 staff; they answer offers under the shop's name; the owner sees who replied | Real shops have more than one person on the phone | L |
| **VAT invoice on the deal** ⚖️ | A shop with a VAT number can attach a simple invoice to the deal summary | Business buyers need invoices (Bahrain VAT 10%) | M |

## 5. Easy for everyone (access and phones)
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Say it instead of typing** | A 🎤 button on the request form uses the phone's built-in speech-to-text (Arabic or English) | Older users and busy people; no outside service needed in most browsers | S |
| **Share into Unc** | On phones with Unc added to the home screen, "Share → Unc" from Instagram or Photos starts a new request with that picture | People see what they want on Instagram first | M |
| **Bigger text and calm mode** | A setting for larger text and fewer animations | Simple design for all ages | S |
| **Translate a message** ⚖️ | A small "Translate" link under a chat message (Arabic ↔ English, uses a translation service) | Bahrain mixes Arabic, English, Hindi/Urdu, Tagalog speakers | M |

## 6. Feel proud of using Unc
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Saved from waste" counter** | On your profile: "4 second-hand finds — about 60 kg kept out of the bin" (rough per-category figures) | Second-hand is the heart of many requests; makes people feel good | S |
| **Year in review** | In December: "Your 2026 on Unc: 6 requests found, 3 favourite sellers, fastest find 2 hours" — private, shareable if you want | Brings people back once a year, warmly | M |

## Recommended next five (most value for the vision, least risk)
> ✅ All five were built in [[2026-10-22 Session 34]] → [[Privacy, Demand and Trust]].
1. **Who may contact me + first-name-only** — privacy that gets more people posting.
2. **Unmet demand board** — the reverse-marketplace advantage, made visible to shops.
3. **Proof photo with a code** — a simple, strong anti-scam tool.
4. **Warranty / condition / valid-until on offers** — better offers, and Compare offers gets richer.
5. **Polite closing note + "Something went wrong" after a deal** — fair to sellers, safe for buyers.

Next after that: shopping-list requests and borrow/rent (bigger, but new kinds of demand).

## Decisions only the owner can make (⚖️)
- VAT invoices: do we want to handle tax wording at all before launch?
- Translation: which service, and is sending chat text to it acceptable under PDPL? (It would need a clear notice.)
- Giving requests: should Unc ever take part (e.g. a Ramadan campaign with a charity), or stay a neutral place?

## Never (breaks a MUST or trust)
- A "message this shop" button outside a request, even on the QR sticker — the sticker opens the shop page, and contact still starts from a request.
- Showing buyers' names or areas on the unmet demand board.
- Letting shops pay to see requests earlier or to bypass "who may contact me".
