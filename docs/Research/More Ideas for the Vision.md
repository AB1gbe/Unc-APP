# More ideas that fit the vision

Asked in [[2026-10-22 Session 30]]: "different ideas that fit the vision". These are **ideas only**; nothing is built yet. They are different from [[Social Marketplace Ideas]], which was about feeling social and human.

**The vision, in one line:** buyers say what they want; **verified** shops and finders come to them; contact happens **only about a specific request**; simple, Arabic and English, built for Bahrain ([[01 Project Brief]]).
Every idea below was checked against that. Ideas that need a business or legal decision first are marked ⚖️.

**Effort:** S = a day or less · M = 2–4 days · L = a week or more.

## 1. Better requests → better offers
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Request helper per category** | Posting a phone asks "model? storage? condition?"; a car part asks "make, model, year". The answers become neat chips on the request. | Sellers know exactly what's wanted → fewer back-and-forth questions, better offers | M |
| **"What do deals usually cost?"** | While posting: "Recent deals in Electronics: $250–$380". Worked out from **accepted offer prices** (offer cards already record them). | Realistic budgets → more offers; data only Unc has | M |
| **Request health tips** | "Requests with a photo get 2× more offers. Add one?" and "No offers after 2 days: widen your budget or area?" | Turns silence into a next step, with no spam | S |
| **"Still looking?" expiry** | Requests close by themselves after 30 days; 3 days before, the buyer gets "Still looking? Keep it open" (one tap). | Browse stays fresh: sellers stop wasting offers on stale requests | S |
| **Urgency flag** | "Need it today / this week / no rush" on a request; sellers can filter by it | Same-day finders are a real service in Bahrain | S |

## 2. Make choosing easy for buyers
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compare offers side by side** | On your request: a table of every offer with its price, the seller's rating, reply time, badges, photo, and "delivers?". Tap to open the chat or pick. | The core moment of a reverse marketplace: choosing between people who came to you | M |
| **Delivery or pickup on the offer** | The seller ticks "I deliver (fee BD 1)" or "Pickup in Seef"; shown on the offer card | Removes the most common question | S |
| **Arrange pickup card** | After an Accept: "Propose a time and place" (date, time, area); the other person confirms; "Add to calendar" | Moves the deal to completion inside the request chat | M |
| **Safe meetup spots** | The pickup card suggests well-lit public places (malls, police-station safe zones) plus a 3-line safety checklist | Trust is the MUST; it's also what newcomers worry about | S |

## 3. Tools for sellers (shops and finders)
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Request alerts** *(the roadmap's "saved searches")* | "Tell me about new Electronics requests in Manama under BD 150": in-app, push or a daily email | The supply side of a reverse marketplace lives on these alerts | M |
| **Seller dashboard** | Offers sent, accepted %, average reply time, views on your shop page, deals this month | Shops treat Unc as a sales channel | M |
| **Offer templates** | Saved snippets: "Original, 6-month warranty, delivery in 24 h" → one tap inserts it into an offer | Fast, consistent offers from busy shops | S |
| **"Not for me" on requests** | Sellers hide requests they'll never answer, so Home and alerts learn from it | Cleaner lists, better matching | S |

## 4. Trust and safety (beyond KYC)
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Block a member** *(on the roadmap)* | From a chat: that member can no longer contact your requests, and you don't see theirs | A basic safety tool every marketplace needs | S |
| **Scam warnings in chat** | A gentle banner when a message asks for a bank transfer before meeting, shares an IBAN, or pushes a link: "Never pay before you've seen the item" | Protects buyers without reading people's chats by hand | S–M |
| **Deal summary** | After a deal: a page (and PDF) with item, price, date, and both verified names, for warranty or disputes | KYC makes this worth something | S |
| **Response promise for shops** | Shops can show "Replies within 1 hour" only if their real reply time backs it up (uses the existing reply-time data) | Honest signals, not marketing | S |
| ⚖️ **CR check for shops** *(on the roadmap)* | The business number is checked against Sijilat (active or not) and the badge says "CR verified" | Stronger shop trust; needs data access | M |

## 5. Built for Bahrain
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Governorate and area picker** | Choose Capital / Muharraq / Northern / Southern, then an area, instead of typing a city. Powers "near you", alerts and the feed. | Typos split cities today ("Manama", "manama city") | M |
| **BHD done properly** *(on the roadmap)* | Default currency BD with 3 decimals (BD 12.500) | Local money looks local | S |
| **Arabic search that forgives** | "ايفون" finds "iPhone"; أ/ا/إ and ة/ه are treated as the same letter; common English↔Arabic product words | Most Bahraini users mix both languages | M |
| ⚖️ **eKey sign-in / identity** *(on the roadmap)* | National eKey for identity | The gold standard locally; needs eligibility | L |

## 6. Bring people back (without spam)
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly digest email** | For sellers: "12 new requests in your categories this week". For buyers: "Your request got 9 views and no offers: tips". Off by default, one switch in Account. | Re-engages quietly; uses data you already have | S–M |
| **Request again** | On a finished request: "Need it again?" makes a copy in one tap (refills, parts, services) | Repeat needs are common for services | S |
| **Seasonal prompts** | Before Ramadan: "Looking for majlis furniture or a caterer?"; back-to-school in September | Local rhythm; builds on the season code that already exists | S |

## 7. Smart help (optional, uses an AI service) ⚖️
| Idea | What it looks like | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Snap a photo, get a request** | Take a photo of what you want; Unc suggests the title, category and details for you to check | Posting becomes 10 seconds | M |
| **Write my offer** | A seller taps "Help me write": a clear, polite offer in Arabic or English from a few words | Better offers from people who don't like writing | S–M |

These cost money per use, and they send photos or text to an AI provider. They need a privacy line in the terms first.

## 8. Earning money, without breaking trust ⚖️
| Option | How it works | Fits the vision? |
|---|---|---|
| **Shop Pro subscription** | The dashboard, alerts with more filters, offer templates, and a featured shop page | ✅ Pays for tools, not for trust |
| **Pay per offer (lead credits)** | Shops buy credits to answer requests (the Thumbtack model) | ⚠️ Fewer junk offers, but may scare new shops; needs testing |
| **Buyer protection (escrow via BenefitPay)** | The buyer pays into Unc, and the seller is paid once the buyer confirms | ✅ Strong trust; needs a licensed payment partner |
| ❌ **Paid ranking of offers or "verified" for money** | — | Breaks trust; on the never list |

## Recommended next five (most value for the vision)
1. **Compare offers side by side**: makes the core choice easy.
2. **Request alerts for sellers**: the engine that brings offers in.
3. **Governorate and area picker**: fixes location for everything that uses it.
4. **Block a member + scam warnings**: safety basics before launch.
5. **"Still looking?" expiry + request health tips**: keeps the market fresh and helpful.

## Decisions only the owner can make
- Monetization (section 8): which model, if any, and when.
- AI features (section 7): yes or no, and which provider.
- Legal or partner work: Sijilat CR checks, eKey, BenefitPay escrow.
