# Bahrain Market Research: is there anything like Unc?

> Asked in [[2026-10-21 Session 10]]: *"Is there anything similar to the app we are building here in Bahrain?"*
> **Method:** web searches by a research agent (an AI working for Claude), not direct visits to the live sites. The session's network proxy blocked opening app-store pages, so download numbers come from search snippets and third-party trackers.
> **Labels:** **[Verified]** = stated by the cited source · **[Inferred]** = reasoning, not stated anywhere · **[Unconfirmed]** = couldn't be checked. Check anything important yourself before relying on it.

## Short answer
- **No direct equivalent was found in Bahrain** [Inferred: not found in the searches, which doesn't prove nothing exists]. Nothing combines all of Unc's core features:
  - any buyer posts what they *want*;
  - shops and freelancers reply **privately about that one request**;
  - **every member is identity-verified**;
  - both sides rate each other.
- **What exists are pieces of the idea:**
  - "wanted to buy" ads in general classifieds sites (Mourjan, OpenSooq);
  - quote apps for one category (HomeX and Mismaar for home repairs, Garage Bahrain for car parts);
  - a request platform for businesses only (Procural);
  - Mrsool, where couriers bid on errands.
- **Where Bahrainis buy and sell today:**
  - **dubizzle Bahrain** (the former OLX Bahrain);
  - **OpenSooq**;
  - Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook groups.
- **Scams are a documented problem.** Police and the Interior Ministry have warned about them, which supports Unc's "every member verified" promise.

## 3. Bahrain-specific facts that affect Unc
### Identity verification (KYC)
- **eKey 2.0** is Bahrain's national digital ID, run by the iGA (Information & eGovernment Authority) and built with **Beyon Connect** [Verified].
- **EKEY for Business (EKEY-B)** is the version private companies can use: identity checks with the user's consent, plus biometrics. Beyon Connect is the **only reseller** to the private sector [Verified]. Every company found using it is financial (KFH, CrediMax, LuLu Exchange and others). **Whether a marketplace like Unc can join, and at what price, is unconfirmed: ask Beyon Connect.**
- The **CBB eKYC platform** and the **bulk KYC service** are for **licensed financial institutions** [Verified], so probably not available to Unc [Inferred].
- **Uqudo** (Middle East digital identity: reads the ID chip, checks the person is live on camera) [Verified]. **Whether it, Sumsub and similar services support the Bahrain CPR chip is unconfirmed.**
- **For Unc** [Inferred]:
  1. **Now:** keep the current manual review, or switch to a document-plus-selfie service.
  2. **Later:** apply for EKEY-B to offer "verified with eKey".
  - See [[KYC Verification]] and [[ADR-005 KYC approach]].

### Business registration (CR on Sijilat)
- **Sijilat** (Ministry of Industry & Commerce) has a public search by CR number or name showing status and activities [Verified]. **No public API found**, and scraping may break its terms.
- Cancelled or lapsed CRs stay in the register, so check the **status**, not just that the number exists [Verified].
- The low-cost virtual CR (**Sijili**) has included "retail sale via internet" since 2020 [Verified]. So requiring "a CR or Sijili" from shop accounts is reasonable, and Instagram sellers can get one [Inferred].
- **For Unc** [Inferred]: ask shop accounts for their CR number, and have the admin check it on Sijilat (status active, activity matches) and record the date. This could replace most of the uploaded-document check.

### Laws
- **PDPL (Law 30 of 2018)**, Bahrain's personal data protection law, in force since Aug 2019 [Verified]. Its 2022 rules cover:
  - sending data abroad;
  - security measures;
  - "sensitive" data;
  - registering a data protection officer.
- ID documents and biometrics are probably sensitive data, and hosting them outside Bahrain triggers the transfer rules [Inferred]. **Get legal advice.**
- **Online shops** must show their contact details and CR number, and publish return and privacy policies. The Ministry enforced this in January 2025 [Verified].
- **Open question:** what CR activity Unc itself needs as a platform that doesn't sell goods. Ask the Ministry (MOIC).

### Payments, language, market size
- **BenefitPay / BENEFIT:**
  - 2025: 466M transactions worth BD 10.2B across its transfer services.
  - First half of 2026: 246M transactions worth BD 5.4B (+7.2%) [Verified].
  - It is the default way to pay a person; cash on meeting is still common for second-hand items [Inferred].
- **People:**
  - about 1.60M population;
  - 99% internet use;
  - about 1.2M smartphone users (estimate) [Verified].
- **Language:** Arabic and English. The leading classifieds sites are bilingual with right-to-left Arabic [Inferred].

## 4. What Unc would need to compete in Bahrain [Inferred]
1. **Arabic-first, right-to-left design**, plus English.
2. **BHD currency** with 3 decimals (fils). Currently the app offers USD/EUR/GBP/CAD/AUD.
3. **BenefitPay** for payments or deposits, through a licensed payment provider. Cash on meeting stays allowed.
4. **Instant notifications:** phone push notifications, and maybe WhatsApp alerts that link back into the request's conversation (chat stays inside Unc, per MUST #3).
5. **eKey (EKEY-B)** as the long-term identity check; ID document plus selfie until then.
6. **CR check on Sijilat** for shops.
7. **PDPL compliance:** consent, a data protection officer, where the data is hosted, never showing full CPR numbers (Unc already stores only the last 4).
8. **Getting the first sellers:** with about 1.6M people, early supply is key. Invite Instagram shops, phone and electronics stores, parts suppliers and home-service providers.
9. **Share a request to WhatsApp or Instagram**, so buyers bring demand from where people already are.

## Gap Unc could fill [Inferred]
- **Trust:** every member is verified and every shop's CR is checked, addressing the documented scam problem.
- **Privacy:** the buyer's phone number isn't public like on classifieds sites, and there's no cold-messaging.
- **One app for any want:** electronics, parts, furniture and services together, instead of separate single-category apps.
- **A way for Instagram shops to find customers.**
