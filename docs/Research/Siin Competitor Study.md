# Siin (سين): competitor study, and where Unc stands

**Asked (session 55):** "Study the application called Siin, all the details, what it looks like, their unique offering and position, and then tell me mine."

**Method and limits:** web searches (the App Store page and news articles couldn't be opened from this session: network block), then **5 screenshots of the app sent by the owner** (section 6). Company figures are their own claims.

## 1. What Siin is
| | Siin |
|---|---|
| **Type** | **Live-stream shopping and auctions** ("live commerce"): sellers go live on video, show items, buyers bid or buy in real time |
| **Tagline (App Store)** | "Live Shopping \| بيع وشراء مباشر": shopping and selling made "easy, social and exciting" |
| **Focus** | Rare items and collectibles across several categories, high-value items |
| **Hooks** | Real-time bidding, giveaways, competitions ("gamified" shopping) |
| **Trust** | Says it connects users with **verified sellers and buyers** |
| **Payments** | In-app: Apple Pay, Visa/Mastercard, Mada |
| **Delivery** | Across the GCC |
| **Markets** | Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman (all six "in under a year") |
| **Origin / base** | Born in **Bahrain**: won 1st place at the 8th StartUp Bahrain Pitch (Nov 2023), stc Bahrain inspireU accelerator (2024). Seller on the App Store: *SIIN Ecommerce Company W.L.L*. Reports now place its base in **Riyadh** |
| **Founders** | Hesham AlSaati (co-founder); another co-founder named Ahmed Allawi/Alawi in some outlets |
| **Funding** | **$3 million total** (May 2026 round led by **VentureSouq** and **Shift Group**, with Plus VC, Oqal and angels) |
| **Traction (company claims)** | "Hundreds of thousands" of items sold, "tens of thousands of hours" of live streams; App Store 4.8★ from 47 ratings (at the time of the search) |
| **Weak spots seen** | Some user reviews complain about support and how disputes are handled (single reviews; anecdotal) |

## 2. Siin's unique offering and position
- **Offering:** *entertainment + scarcity*. Shopping is a **show**: a live host, a countdown, bids, giveaways. It works best for items people *want* (collectibles, rare items, trends) and for impulse buying.
- **Position:** "The Gulf's live / social commerce platform": **seller-led** (the seller decides what's sold and when), **broadcast** (one seller to many viewers), **regional** (GCC-wide, delivery and in-app payment).
- **Moat:** a community of hosts and regular viewers, in-app payments and logistics, funding to grow GCC-wide.

## 3. Unc's offering and position (from what's built)
| | **Unc** | **Siin** |
|---|---|---|
| Who starts | **The buyer**: "I need X" (reverse marketplace) | The seller: "watch what I'm selling" |
| Why people come | A **need**, often hard to find (a part, a used console, a service, a table that fits) | Fun, discovery, rare items, bidding |
| Shape | **One buyer ↔ a few sellers, privately**, only inside that request's chat | One host ↔ many viewers, in public |
| Price | **Sellers compete with offers**; the buyer compares (Compare offers, offer details, proof photos) | Bids go **up** (auction) or the seller's price |
| Trust | **Identity check (KYC) for everyone**, card on file, ratings only from real deals, proof photos, scam warnings, block/report, "something went wrong" help | Verified sellers/buyers (details unknown), in-app payment |
| Privacy | Numbers hidden until a price is agreed, first-name-only, who-may-contact-me | Public streams |
| Categories | **Anything incl. services** (assembly, repairs, finders) | Mostly physical products, collectibles |
| Market | **Bahrain first** (areas, Arabic, governorates), then GCC | GCC-wide |
| Payments / delivery | Not yet (paying in parts between buyer and seller; Tap planned) | Built in |
| Stage | Working product, not launched; no funding | Funded ($3M), live in 6 markets |

**Unc's unique offering, in one line:**
> **"Tell Unc what you need; verified sellers come to you with offers, privately."**
The buyer is in charge, the search is done by the sellers, and every contact is about one real need, with identity checks on both sides.

**Unc's position:** the **trusted, need-first marketplace for Bahrain**: for things you *need* (not things you happen to see), where shops and finders compete for your request instead of shouting into a feed. Calm and private, where Siin is loud and public.

## 4. Are they competitors?
**Mostly not head-on.** Siin wins *"I want to be entertained and maybe buy something rare"*; Unc wins *"I need this specific thing, find it for me"*.
- **Overlap:** both are Bahrain-born C2C/B2C marketplaces with verified members; both court the same shops (a Siin seller could also answer Unc requests), and both compete for the same users' attention and app space.
- **Where Siin is ahead:** payments and delivery in the app; GCC scale; funding; a community and habit (watching streams).
- **Where Unc is ahead:** services and any kind of need; stronger identity and privacy (KYC for all, hidden numbers, request-bound chats); buyer comparison tools; Bahrain-specific detail (governorates, Arabic first-class, demand board for shops).

## 5. What Unc can learn (without copying the live-show model)
1. **Make the moment exciting:** a short "offers are coming in" view on a new request (live count, first offer celebration); Siin shows people love the thrill.
2. **Payments in the app** (Tap / BenefitPay) is a must-have to compete for trust; it's already on the roadmap.
3. **Shops as hosts:** let a shop show a **short video** with an offer (like a mini live demo): fits Unc's private, request-bound rule.
4. **Use Unc's demand data as the pitch to shops:** "Siin gives you an audience; Unc gives you buyers who already asked."
5. **Possible partnership instead of fight:** Siin sellers answering Unc requests, or "can't find it on a stream? Ask on Unc".

**Overall look:** dark theme (near-black), **purple** accent, bold white text, red "Live" badges. It feels like a gaming or streaming app (Twitch-like), not a shop. Two-column picture grids everywhere. Bottom tabs: **Lives · Marketplace · Sell (+) · Orders · Account**.

**1. Lives (the home screen)**
- Top: "Search Siin", a purple 🎁 **Drops** button (rewards), chat, notifications, cart.
- Category tiles with **live counters**: "All, 7 Lives Now", "TCG, 6 Lives Now", "Currencies & Stamps, 1 Live Now", "Gaming".
- A grid of live shows: seller **@handle + country flag** (mostly 🇸🇦 Saudi, 🇦🇪 UAE), a big thumbnail with a red **Live** badge, extras like "🔥 On fire" and "🎁 Cashback giveaways", a show title ("30th Anniversary Celebration 🎉", "Daily Show: Rayquaza Hunt") and its category.
- Content seen: **Pokémon and One Piece trading cards (TCG), coins and stamps** (an Arabic coin-auction shop "متجر مزاد الباسل للعملات").

**2. Marketplace (listings, when not live)**
- Tabs: **For You, Gaming, Comics, Tickets, TCG, Currencies**. Filters: Type, Sort, **Auction 🔥**, **Ending soon**.
- Cards: seller @handle + flag, photo, ♡ save, a Drops badge on some, an orange **countdown** ("24:31") for auctions, title (Arabic or English), price **in the viewer's currency (BHD)**, "No bids".
- Items seen: a children's magazine issue, a One Piece promo card, PS5/PS4 games (Elden Ring, Sleeping Dogs).
- Header: favourites, recently viewed, cart.

**3. Sell**
- A bottom sheet "Start Selling on Siin": *Easy to Start (no selling experience needed) · Go Live or Sell on Marketplace (live shows or a shop) · Start Earning (convert your inventory into income)* → **Get Started**.

**4. Orders**
- In Progress / Completed. Siin handles the order end to end (payment, then delivery).

**5. Account**
- An auto-made username ("ancient_box180"), no real name. **Get Help → WhatsApp**.
- A banner for an event partnership: **TCG Con Arabia**, Riyadh, 4–5 Dec 2026 (tickets).
- Tiles: **My Wallet** (balance BHD 0), **Offers** ("manage & track your offers": buyers can offer below a seller's price), **Drops** ("3 Drops available"), **My Bids** (outbids / winning).
- **Refer a Friend**, Category preferences, **Following** (sellers), **My Clips** (short videos from shows).

## 7. What the screens change in the picture
- **Siin is a collectors' community**, not a general marketplace: trading cards, coins and stamps, video games, comics, event tickets. Its users are hobbyists who come back daily for shows ("Daily Show"), drops and bids. **Even less overlap with Unc** than the news suggested.
- **Identity is light on the surface:** handles and flags, no real names or "verified" badges visible on cards. Unc shows real (or first) names, ✓ Verified, ratings and badges everywhere.
- **Siin's "Offers" go the other way from Unc's:** a buyer offers a lower price on a seller's item. On Unc, sellers make offers on a buyer's need.
- **Money stays inside:** wallet, cart, orders, cashback. Unc has none of that yet (Tap is planned).
- **Support is on WhatsApp**, outside the app; Unc has in-app reports and "Something went wrong".
- **Habits:** live counters, countdowns, drops, cashback, referrals, following, clips. These are all ways to bring people back every day.

## 8. Unc vs Siin, the look
| | Siin | Unc |
|---|---|---|
| Feel | Dark, purple, loud, game/stream-like | Light (dark mode follows the phone), blue, calm, clean |
| Home | Live shows to watch | Your requests, requests you can help with, what's happening near you |
| Centre tab | **Sell** (+) | **Post a request** (+) |
| Cards | Thumbnail + @handle + flag + price/countdown | Photo + category + budget + area + offers count |
| People | Handles | Names (or "Sam B."), ✓ Verified, ★ ratings, badges |
| Languages | English UI with Arabic content | Full English **and Arabic (right-to-left)** |

**Where Unc's position is sharper after seeing Siin:**
> Siin is where **collectors** go to **watch and win**. Unc is where **anyone** goes to **ask and get** what they need: everyday items, parts, furniture, services, from verified people in Bahrain.

## 9. Ideas worth borrowing (fit Unc's rules)
> ✅ Ideas 3–5 were built in [[2026-11-08 Session 58]] → [[Orders, Wallet, Invites and Video]].
> ✅ Ideas 1 and 2 were built in [[2026-11-08 Session 56]] → [[Social Touches#8. Live counters and "Closes in N days" (session 56)]].
1. **Live counters on Home and Browse:** "12 sellers active now · 5 requests posted today" (real numbers only).
2. **Countdown on requests** that close soon ("closes in 2 days"): Unc already has expiry ([[ADR-037 Expiry and tips]]); show it on cards.
3. **Refer a friend**, with a small reward once Unc earns money.
4. **A wallet and orders** once Tap is connected (paying in parts, protection).
5. **Short video with an offer** (like Siin's clips), private to the request's chat.
6. **Get Help** button, but in-app (Unc already has reports and deal help).
7. Don't copy: public live chat between strangers, or handles instead of verified names. Both go against Unc's trust promise.
