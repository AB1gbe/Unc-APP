## Part 2: "add the colour switcher to the test site"
- `client/src/palettes.js`:
  - `PALETTES` holds Now and A–E;
  - `applyPalette()` sets the CSS colour tokens and the logo fill;
  - the choice is kept in `localStorage` under `unc.palette`.
- `client/src/components/ColourSwitcher.jsx`: a floating "Colours" button above the tab bar opens the list. It is mounted in `client/src/components/Layout.jsx`.
- `client/src/main.jsx` applies a saved choice before the first paint.
- `server/src/app.js` `/api/meta`: `colourSwitcher` is true on the test site (`TEST_SITE=1`) and in development only, never on a real site.
- `client/src/styles.css` gets `.palette-*`; Arabic is added in `ar.js`.
- Checks:

  - devices OK (after fixing M163);
  - smoke OK;
