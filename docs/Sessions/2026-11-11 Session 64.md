# 2026-11-11 Session 64: Real product photos (blocked: no image credits)

## What was asked
"Use Claude to generate real product photos" (to replace the drawn pictures from [[2026-11-10 Session 62]]).

## What we found
- Claude itself writes text and code; it can't paint photos. The drawn pictures come from `scripts/make-art.mjs`.
- The connected image service (Higgsfield) can make realistic photos. A cost check said **0.25 credits per photo** (model `gpt_image_2_6`, 4:3).
- The account has **0 credits** (free plan), and free trial generations are not available. So **no photos were made and nothing was spent.**

## What it would take
About 30 photos for everything (11 demo photos, 16 categories, 3 "How it works" steps): roughly **7–8 credits**. After buying credits, ask again and the photos can be made and dropped into:
- `server/demo-photos/` (same file names), then `npm run seed` on a fresh database;
- `client/public/art/` (same file names as now).

Other ways: send your own photos (that you have the rights to), or allow a free photo site (e.g. Unsplash) on Claude's work machine.
