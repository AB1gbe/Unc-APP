# 2026-11-10 Session 62: A visual, grown-up look

## What was asked
1. "Why does it look full of writing only, no images, nothing… it looks bland and boring… visual is the best medium."
2. After the first pass: "now it looks too childish, I need something like Siin or something like this", with a screenshot of dark tiles (a single bowl on a brown tile, a street photo, serif "Collections" heading, orange "See all").

## What we did, in order
1. Looked at the app as a visitor, a member and on a phone. The banner, "How it works", the Help page and cards without photos were text only.
2. **First pass:** bright pictures (front-page collage, three steps, 16 category tiles, Help pictures) and category pictures on cards without a photo. The owner found it **too childish** → [[Mistakes and Fixes#M115]].
3. **Second pass (pushed, `0753da1`):**
   - dark theme for everyone, coral accent, Fraunces / Inter / Noto Kufi Arabic fonts;
   - pictures redrawn: one object on a deep, muted colour; demo photos in the same tones;
   - the banner shows the newest real requests as cards instead of a drawing;
   - Help page with a banner, topic tiles and pictures.
4. Tried to download real sample photos (Unsplash, Pexels): blocked by this machine's network, so none are used.
5. Arabic top-bar button wrapped with the new font → fixed ([[Mistakes and Fixes#M116]]).

Checks: `npm test` 187 · E2E OK · ARABIC E2E OK · DEVICES OK (5 sizes × 2 languages × 17 pages).

## Important for the owner
- `pull` runs `npm install` by itself (new font packages).
- Pictures are drawn by `scripts/make-art.mjs`. To use your own photos instead, put them in `client/public/art/` with the same names.
