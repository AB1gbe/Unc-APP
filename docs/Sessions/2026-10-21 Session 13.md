# 2026-10-21 Session 13 — Images, real profiles, a whole user experience

## What was asked
"Add the ability to add images, create a user — make it look like a whole user experience, not just buying and selling fast and getting out."

## How we read it (plan, built in this order, each step pushed separately)
1. **Profile photo + a real profile**: photo, short bio, what you buy/sell (interests), languages, edit-profile page; public profile shows it all.
2. **Welcome / onboarding** after sign-up: add a photo, a bio and interests (skippable).
3. **Home for logged-in members**: what needs your attention (new offers, replies, deals to rate), your requests at a glance, and "requests for you" matching your interests.
4. **Images everywhere**: photos in chat messages; add/remove photos when editing a request (up to 6); full-screen photo viewer. Photos are shrunk in the browser before upload (faster on phones).
5. **Saved requests** (♥) so sellers can come back later.
6. **Notifications** (🔔): new offer, new message, deal, rating, identity check result.

Rules that stay: contact only through a request (profiles have no message button); KYC photos are never reused or shown.
