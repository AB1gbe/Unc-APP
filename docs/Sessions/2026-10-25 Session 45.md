# 2026-10-25 Session 45: Can it go on the Play Store for free?

## What was asked
"Is it not possible to install it on the Play Store?" (after [[Step 53 - Test as an app on your phone for free]]).

## Answer (also added to [[Publishing as an App]])
- Not for free. Any Play Store listing, even a private "internal test", needs a **Google Play developer account ($25 once)**.
- It also needs a **fixed https address**. The free tunnel changes address every time, so it fits phone testing but not the Play Store. A free hosting tier (fixed `…onrender.com` address) is enough for a test.
- Cheapest Play Store test: $25 once + free hosting + PWABuilder + **Internal testing** (up to 100 invited testers).
- Free alternative: the test `.apk` from PWABuilder (Step 53), installed directly on Android phones.
