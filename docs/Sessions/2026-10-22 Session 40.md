- The **Terms are a draft**. A lawyer in Bahrain must review them (consumer law, PDPL, e-commerce rules) before launch.

- **Paying in parts:** money goes directly between buyer and seller; Unc does not lend. An installment partner comes later ([[ADR-052 Paying in parts]], [[Roadmap]]).
