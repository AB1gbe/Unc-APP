# 2026-10-22 Session 39: Keeping deals on Unc (people swapping numbers)

## What was asked
"How do we ensure that the transactions are ongoing in our app and not them just taking each other's number and contacting them privately?"

## What we did
1. Checked the code:
   - `server/src/lib/contact-guard.js` blocks numbers, links, emails and @handles on **profiles and shop pages** only;
   - **chat messages are not checked**;
   - the scam warning (`client/src/scam.js`) does not look for phone numbers.
2. Wrote [[Keeping Deals on Unc]] (`docs/Research/`). It covers:
   - why no marketplace can stop this completely;
   - the business model choice that makes it matter less (earn per offer or by shop subscription, not commission);
   - the reasons to stay that are already built;
   - light friction we can add (hide numbers until a price is agreed, a nudge, catch tricks, count attempts, measure the leak);
   - what not to do.
3. Linked it from [[Roadmap]] and [[00 Home]].
4. No code changes, and nothing decided yet, so no ADR.
